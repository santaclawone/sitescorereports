Title tags

The title tag is an HTML element that specifies the title of a web page. It appears in search results, browser tabs, and social share previews. It is the single most important on-page SEO factor.

Why title tags matter

Search engines use the title tag as the primary signal for what a page is about. It appears as the clickable headline in search results. A well-written title tag directly impacts your click-through rate and search rankings.

For local Irish businesses, the title tag is often the first impression a potential customer has of your site. A clear, descriptive title helps them decide whether to click your result or a competitor's.

Title tag best practices

Place your primary keyword near the beginning. Keep titles under 60 characters � longer titles get truncated in search results, usually at around 580 pixels wide. Use your brand name at the end, separated by a pipe or dash.

Example: Pressure Washing Dublin | Reliable Commercial & Residential Cleaning. This title leads with the service, includes the location, and ends with the brand.

Common mistakes

Duplicate title tags across multiple pages confuse search engines. Missing title tags are a critical error � every page needs a unique, descriptive title. Titles that are too short waste valuable real estate. Keyword stuffing hurts readability and may trigger spam filters.

Default titles like 'Home' or 'Untitled Document' signal neglect. Each page on your site should have a hand-crafted title that accurately describes its content.

Title tags and the audits

Your site report checks every page for title tag presence, length, uniqueness, and keyword placement. Pages with missing or duplicate titles are flagged as high priority issues.

Tip: Write titles for humans who are searching, not for search engine algorithms. A title that sounds natural and compelling will outperform a keyword-stuffed alternative every time.
